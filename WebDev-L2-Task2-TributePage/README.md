# Virat Kohli Tribute Page 🏏

A responsive tribute website dedicated to **Virat Kohli**, one of India's most celebrated cricketers. This project highlights his cricketing journey, achievements, records, leadership, and entrepreneurial ventures through a modern dark-themed design.

## 🌟 Features

- **Hero Section** — Introduction to Virat Kohli.
- **About Section** — His background and cricketing journey.
- **Career Timeline** — Key milestones throughout his career.
- **Career Statistics** — Highlights of his cricketing achievements.
- **IPL Journey** — His journey in the Indian Premier League.
- **Achievements & Awards** — Major milestones and honours.
- **Records** — Notable accomplishments in international cricket.
- **Brands & Business Ventures** — Highlights of one8, One8 Commune, and WROGN, with descriptions of Kohli's association with each.
- **Legacy Section** — His influence on cricket and aspiring athletes.
- **Responsive Design** — Layout designed for desktop and mobile screens.
- **Navigation** — Easy access to the website's main sections.

## 💼 Brands & Business Ventures

The website features three ventures associated with Virat Kohli:

- **one8** — A lifestyle and sportswear brand co-created by Kohli, now part of Agilitas Sports.
- **One8 Commune** — A restaurant and hospitality venture associated with the one8 brand.
- **WROGN** — A youth-focused fashion brand co-created with Kohli.

Their inclusion reflects his entrepreneurial activities; it does not imply that he solely owns every venture.
