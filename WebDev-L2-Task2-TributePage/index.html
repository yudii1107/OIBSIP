01 / THE PERSON

More than a cricketer.

Some players make runs. Some players change the energy of a team. Virat Kohli has built a career around doing both.

Born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India, Kohli rose through age-group cricket before captaining India to the 2008 ICC Under-19 World Cup title. He made his senior international debut in 2008 and became known for his intensity, fitness, run-chasing and remarkable consistency.

He has represented India across formats, captained the national side, become one of the greatest one-day international batters, and built a lasting connection with Royal Challengers Bengaluru in the IPL.

His story is not only about records. It is about preparation, resilience, ambition and the willingness to keep improving.

02 / THE JOURNEY

Every era. Another milestone.

A look at the moments that helped shape an extraordinary career.

2008

THE BEGINNING

A captain's first triumph

Led India to the ICC Under-19 World Cup title, made his ODI debut, and began his long association with Royal Challengers Bengaluru.

2011

WORLD CHAMPION

A dream realised

Was part of India's victorious 2011 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup team and scored a century in the tournament's group stage.

2013

THE CHASE MASTER

A global force

Helped India win the ICC Champions Trophy and continued establishing himself as one of the world's leading ODI batters.

2018

A PEAK ERA

Dominance across formats

Delivered outstanding batting performances around the world and received the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna, India's highest sporting honour at the time.

2023

HISTORY REWRITTEN

The 50th ODI century

Scored his 50th ODI hundred during the World Cup semi-final and finished the tournament with a record 765 runs.

2024

WORLD CHAMPION AGAIN

A T20 World Cup farewell

Played a match-winning innings in the T20 World Cup final as India lifted the trophy, then announced his retirement from T20 internationals.

2025

ANOTHER ICC TITLE

Champions Trophy glory

Helped India win the 2025 ICC Champions Trophy and retired from Test cricket later that year, continuing his ODI career.

2026

THE RCB LEGACY

Back-to-back IPL titles

RCB retained the IPL title in 2026. Kohli contributed 675 runs during the season, continuing his long-standing journey with the franchise.

04 / THE IPL LEGACY

One team. Since day one.

Kohli's long association with Royal Challengers Bengaluru has become one of the defining stories of the IPL.

ROYAL CHALLENGERS BENGALURU

From young prospect to franchise icon.

Kohli has represented RCB since the inaugural IPL season in 2008. His loyalty, record-breaking batting and connection with the supporters have made him central to the franchise's identity.

RCB won its first IPL title in 2025 and successfully defended it in 2026. Kohli remained a major contributor to the team's batting in the title-defending season.
